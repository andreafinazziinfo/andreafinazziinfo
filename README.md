### 🧠 Operational Philosophy

> "Simplicity is the ultimate sophistication, but performance is the ultimate necessity."

I engineer software at the intersection of **quantitative systems, local AI pipelines, and premium product design**. I believe that internal tools and private developer environments should not only be high-performance, but also beautiful, intuitive, and satisfying to operate. 

My work is **Local-First and Private-by-Default**, focused on robust containerized environments and secure automation workflows.
